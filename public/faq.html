Common Questions

Is it safe to buy accounts ?

Customer safety is at the heart of everything we do. We use Stripe as our payment processor to ensure safety and privacy for all of our customers. This means that all your payment details are processed by Stripe and are not accessible by us. There is no risk whatsoever in buying a League of Legends account from us.

How fast will I receive my LoL account ?

Our fully automated instant delivery system will have your account details delivered to your inbox instantly. No more waiting for your account!

How does this service work?

We connect you to our Selling Users (Boosters & Coaches) and then they deliver their service to you whether it’s elo boosting or coaching.

I have a question which I can't find in the FAQ. How can I ask it?

You can ask us the question by contacting the management.

Can I purchase a service as a guest

Yes, you surely can. If you purchase a service as a guest, you will receive all the information about the service on the e-mail address that you provided at the beginning of the process.

Does Yare own a private Discord server ?

Yes, Yare owns a private Discord server. We always suggest our users to join our Discord.

Where will I get the account information after purchasing it on the website ?

After the payment is completed and verified by the payment processor, your account will be automatically delivered to your profile on the website. After that, you just need to log into your profile on the website, exchange all the information necessary with the seller and completing the transaction. If you purchased account as a guest, you will receive your account information in the e-mail address that you provided at the beginning of the purchase process.

What if I made mistakes on my purchase -What do I do ?

If you did mistaken yourself while purchasing the account, you can talk to our Live Support or go through our contact section on the website. Our live agents are online 24/7 to assist you and provide immediate help in order to fix the problem.

Can I sell League of Legends account ?

You can! If you want to sell a League of Legends account on our website, you are obligated to create an account on our website. After that, you will have to log into your profile and select “marketplace”. In the end, you fill all the required information about the account you want to sell. Within 24 hours, you'll receive an e-mail informing you if Yare approved or disapproved your request.

If you want to request a refund, please go to our Terms of Service and check the specific regulations about the refund options.
